rclone for personal use

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This is a personal app for one person: its owner. It does not collect, sell, or share anyone’s data, and it has no servers of its own.

What the app is

rclone for personal use is a private setup of the open-source rclone command-line program. It syncs files between a folder on the owner’s computer and the owner’s own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the owner signs in to it.

Information it accesses

After the owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app can read and write files and folders in the owner’s Google Drive, and read their metadata such as names, sizes, and modification times. The permissions requested are drive, drive.metadata.readonly, and docs.

The app does not access any other Google service, and it does not access anyone else’s account.

How the information is used

Only to copy and update files between the owner’s computer and the owner’s Google Drive. It is not used for any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

Files travel directly between the owner’s computer and Google. They are not sent to any server operated by the developer. The sign-in token that Google issues is kept in a configuration file on the owner’s computer.

No information is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app uses no analytics or tracking tools.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can remove the app’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the stored token by removing the rclone configuration file from the computer. Files that were already synced stay where they are until the owner deletes them.
